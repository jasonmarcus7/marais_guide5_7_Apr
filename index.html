Art Museum
Musée National Picasso
9 min walk from Rue Braque · ~710m
5 Rue de Thorigny, 75003
Housed in a magnificent 17th-century hôtel particulier, this is one of the world's great museum experiences. Picasso's entire personal collection — paintings, sculptures, prints, and ceramics — displayed across dramatic staircases and gilded rooms. There is also a coffee shop at the top with delicious pastries and rooftop views. Free for EU residents under 26.

Science & Technology
Musée des Arts et Métiers
6 min walk from Rue Braque · ~510m
60 Rue Réaumur, 75003
One of Paris's most underrated institutions — early automobiles, flying machines, and scientific instruments displayed inside a breathtaking deconsecrated medieval church. Foucault's pendulum hangs from the choir vault. Open until 9pm on Fridays, making it a perfect late-afternoon escape when the other museums have closed.

World Museum
14 min walk from Rue de Braque · ~1.1km
Musée du Louvre
Rue de Rivoli, 75001 Paris
One of the world's great cultural pilgrimages — and from Rue de Braque, entirely walkable. Home to the Mona Lisa, Venus de Milo, and Winged Victory, but the real magic is getting lost in the Egyptian antiquities or the Flemish masters long after the crowds have gone. Book tickets in advance. Enter via the metro station to skip the pyramid queues. Free for EU residents under 26, and free to all on the first Friday evening of the month.
